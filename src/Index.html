    User guide

    1. Picking a camera

    • Select your name from the list and enter your PIN, then choose a camera. Use the Site menu to narrow the list.
    • Cameras already claimed by another student are disabled. You can only claim one camera at a time.
    • Select Claim & Begin review. The app remembers your place, so you can close the tab and pick up later.

    2. Reviewing footage

    • Space plays or pauses. Clicking the picture does the same.
    • ← and → move one file at a time.
    • The File Scrub slider jumps anywhere in the folder. Delay sets the pause between images while playing.
    • When reviewing a video, use the Video controls to scrub through the clip or jump forward and back.
    • Scroll or pinch to zoom, and drag to look around. Double-click or Reset zoom puts the picture back.
    • Overlay text chooses which details show on the picture.

    3. Tagging animals

    • Click the Species box and start typing. The best match is highlighted: press Enter to choose it, then Enter again to tag the file.
    • The species stays filled in for the next file, which is handy for a burst of photos of one animal. Esc or Clear empties it.
    • Species you used recently appear as buttons. Click one, or press 1–6, to tag the current file. Press Pin beside a species to lock it in its slot so newer species don't replace it (Pinned unpins it).
    • If you type a species that isn't in the list, the app checks for typos and asks before adding it for everyone.
    • Undo last tag (or Ctrl+Z) puts your most recent tag back.

    4. Fixing an older tag

    • Click Tagged photos to see every photo you have tagged for this camera. Type in the search box to find a species or file name.
    • Click a photo to open it. Use the ← and → buttons or the arrow keys to move through the photos.
    • To change the species, edit the Species box (use + for more than one animal) and click Save new species. The file name and the Master Data log update right away.
    • Remove tag takes the tag off completely. The photo goes back into your review queue as an untagged photo and its row is removed from the Master Data log.

    5. Viewing everyone's tagged photos

    • On the first screen, click View tagged photos and sign in with your name and PIN. You can browse the tagged photos of every completed camera, one camera at a time or all together.
    • Search, sort, or click a photo to open it, then use the arrow keys to move through the photos.
    • Click ☆ Star to favorite a photo. Click Nahhh if you think the ID is wrong, and add the species you think it is. Your instructor can see these and make the official change.

    6. Finishing Up

    • When you have tagged all photos for your assigned camera, click Submit & Finish Review.
    • This marks your camera complete and allows you to sign up for another one.
